# Kin

A static voice assistant page. No backend.

The page holds no keys and no private data. Keys and the imported knowledge pack are stored only in the browser of the device that opens it.

Open the site, allow the microphone once, add your own keys in Settings, and import your pack.

What Kin remembers, what it makes, the logbook, your reminders and your lists stay in that browser too. Web searches and design reviews run at Anthropic, on your key.

Optional: Settings > Pictures > Connect Higgsfield signs in to your own Higgsfield account from this browser (OAuth with PKCE). The sign-in token stays in this browser and is handed to Anthropic's MCP connector only for a picture job you approve. Pictures are priced first, approved once per job, and held to a daily credit cap you set.
