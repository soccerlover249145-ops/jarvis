Brain & keys

Brain

The key is kept in this browser on this device only and is sent to Anthropic and nowhere else. With a key, what you say or type, the last few exchanges, your pack’s rules and brief, and the entries Jarvis looks up are sent to Anthropic to get each answer. Anyone who can use this browser can use the key, so create one just for this, give it a monthly spend limit in the Anthropic Console, and remove it here if the device is lost.

The Gauntlet

A self-test of 15 tasks: math, teaching, writing, and asks Kin should stop at or turn down. It runs only when you tap Run, uses your Claude key, counts toward the limits above, and stops before the budget cap you set (a full run is about $1). Nothing is sent, posted, bought, booked or called. Or say “test yourself, Jarvis”. Results stay on this device.

What Jarvis remembers

Kept in this browser on this device only. Kin adds to it when you say “remember that …”, when you correct an answer, and when you tap Good or Not right under one. It never keeps keys, passwords, your age or school, or family money.

Email

Ask Kin to email someone and it writes the email as a card you can edit. Open in Gmail works with no setup: Gmail opens with it filled in and you press Send there. To send straight from here instead, add your own Google Client ID below; Kin then sends only when you tap Send now twice (once to read it back, once to send).

How to get a Google Client ID (about 5 minutes)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and create a project (any name).
  2. APIs & Services, Library: turn on the Gmail API.
  3. OAuth consent screen: External, keep it in Testing, add your own Gmail address as a test user. Add the scope gmail.send.
  4. Credentials, Create credentials, OAuth client ID, Web application. Authorised JavaScript origin: https://jarvis.unwebbed.app. No redirect URI is needed.
  5. Copy the Client ID (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it above. It is not a secret, but it only works from this site.
  6. For Sync across computers too: in the same project, APIs & Services, Library: turn on the Google Drive API, and add the scope drive.appdata on the consent screen. Jarvis then sees only its own hidden folder, never your other files.

Jarvis can only send (not read) your mail with this, only when you tap, at most the daily number above. The Google permission is kept in memory and forgotten when you close the tab.

One town and one choice of units for the whole page: the morning brief and weather charts both use them. For today’s weather and weather charts, Jarvis asks Open-Meteo (free, no account). Finding your town sends its name to Open-Meteo once; after that only the place’s coordinates are sent. The place is kept on this device.

Morning brief

Say or type “give me my morning brief”. Choose the cards and their order here. Ticks on priorities stay on this device.

With “Start small jobs on my own” on, Jarvis starts at most two small drafts or plans written here from your pack, each under the limit shown, when the brief opens. Research (the web), designs (pictures and credits) and anything that would send, post, buy, book or call always wait for your tap. Market watch uses one short web search at most every three hours, with your Claude key.

Away & desk

Away mode and calls

Say “I’m heading out” to turn away mode on, and “what happened while I was away” for the summary. Say “book a table for three at a place at 7” for a call card. Calls run on a practice line for now: no real call is made until the phone number and the ElevenLabs agent are set up. Anything that would send, post, buy, book, call or delete waits for your tap. Kept on this device only.

Notifications

Grok Bot bridge

Off until you turn it on. When it is on, only a sentence that starts by asking Grok Bot (“ask Grok Bot what is due tomorrow”, typed, or said while Jarvis is awake) is sent. What leaves this device is that question, and pack excerpts only if you pick them for that question. It goes to your relay below, which files it as an issue in your private GitHub mailbox and wakes Grok Bot; Grok Bot’s reply is posted to that issue and read back here. GitHub keeps those issues until someone deletes them. Nothing else from Kin, your pack, your keys or the conversation is sent, and Grok Bot’s replies are shown as its words and are never passed to Claude. Anything Grok Bot would send, post, buy or deploy comes back to you as “needs your OK”.

By default the token is forgotten when this tab closes. If you keep it, anyone who can use this browser profile can ask Grok Bot through your relay until you remove it. Paste it here only, never in a chat.

Sync

Privacy & data

What leaves this device. Your speech goes to the browser maker (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge, Apple in Safari) to be turned into text, whenever the mic is on, asleep or awake. With a Claude key, what you say or type, the last few exchanges, what Jarvis remembers that bears on the question, and the parts of your pack Jarvis uses go to Anthropic; web searches and page reads are run by Anthropic too. With a voice key, every sentence Jarvis speaks goes to ElevenLabs. With Higgsfield connected, the picture prompts of a design job you approve go, through Anthropic, to Higgsfield, and the finished pictures are fetched from Higgsfield into this page. With the Grok Bot bridge turned on, a question you start with “ask Grok Bot” goes to your relay and your private GitHub mailbox, as described under Grok Bot bridge. With Sync turned on, an encrypted copy of what Jarvis remembers, makes and is set to (never keys or the conversation) goes to a hidden folder in your Google Drive, locked with a passphrase only you know. Nothing is ever sent to this website.

Move to another computer

One file with what Jarvis has learned and made here: what it remembers, the Made shelf, the logbook, and your settings for voice, wake phrase, morning brief, away mode and both contact lists, calls and the booking name, weather and limits. Keys, tokens and sign-ins are never in it, so add those again on the other computer. The file is saved by your browser and never uploaded. Keep it private: anyone with it can read what Jarvis remembers.
