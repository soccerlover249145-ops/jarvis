Made

Settings

What leaves this device. Your speech goes to the browser maker (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge, Apple in Safari) to be turned into text, whenever the mic is on, asleep or awake. With a Claude key, what you say or type, the last few exchanges, what Kin remembers that bears on the question, and the parts of your pack Kin uses go to Anthropic; web searches and page reads are run by Anthropic too. With a voice key, every sentence Kin speaks goes to ElevenLabs. With Higgsfield connected, the picture prompts of a design job you approve go, through Anthropic, to Higgsfield, and the finished pictures are fetched from Higgsfield into this page. Nothing is ever sent to this website.

Listening

“Hey Jarvis”, “Jarvis”, “hey Kin” and “wake up” always work too. Say “go to sleep” to rest it and “stop” to cut it off. After an answer Kin stays awake for about 45 seconds; then it goes back to sleep, so talk in the room is not sent as questions.

Voice

Pick a calm British voice from your account’s library.

Optional. The voice key is kept in this browser on this device only. It is sent to ElevenLabs and nowhere else, together with each sentence Kin speaks. Kin says nothing, and sends nothing there, until you have tapped the page once. Anyone who can use this browser can use it, so in ElevenLabs make a key just for this, limit it to Text to Speech and Voices, give it a credit limit, and remove it here if the device is lost.

Brain

The key is kept in this browser on this device only and is sent to Anthropic and nowhere else. With a key, what you say or type, the last few exchanges, your pack’s rules and brief, and the entries Kin looks up are sent to Anthropic to get each answer. Anyone who can use this browser can use the key, so create one just for this, give it a monthly spend limit in the Anthropic Console, and remove it here if the device is lost.

Hard jobs

Documents, plans, designs and decisions go to the strongest model and think harder. Fable 5.2 is the default: Anthropic’s most capable model, about twice the price of Opus 5.5 per word. Choose Opus 5.5 to spend less. Ordinary questions use Opus 5.5 either way.

What Kin remembers

Kept in this browser on this device only. Kin adds to it when you say “remember that …”, when you correct an answer, and when you tap Good or Not right under one. It never keeps keys, passwords, your age or school, or family money.

Kin pack

The pack is a file you export from the Atlas. It is stored in this browser on this device and is never uploaded to this website. With a Claude key, its rules and brief and the entries Kin looks up are sent to Anthropic to answer you.
